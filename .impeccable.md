## Design Context

### Users
The owner uses this Mac desktop app to chat with and manage a persistent local agent team.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, friendly. Preserve the Bot character artwork.

### Aesthetic Direction
Flat, edge-aligned sidebar with a neutral solid background and individual toolbar buttons, following the owner’s supplied chat-app reference. Avoid floating sidebar frames and stacked glass. The owner explicitly prefers softer highlights, consistent rounded controls, and a sidebar aligned with macOS traffic lights. Support light and dark themes.

### Design Principles
- Use subtle glass on functional chrome and menus.
- Keep Bot selection surfaces quiet and role labels free of nested boxes.
- Preserve readable content, keyboard focus, and reduced transparency fallbacks.
- Maintain consistent circular actions and comfortable hit targets.
